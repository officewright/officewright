# Commercial licensing

Officewright is source-available under the [Business Source License 1.1](LICENSE).
Anyone may read, change and redistribute the code, and use it to develop, test and
evaluate software. Production use is free when it meets the Additional Use Grant in
[LICENSE](LICENSE); any other production use needs a commercial license.

## Do I need a commercial license?

| Your situation | Commercial license |
|---|---|
| You develop, test or evaluate software with Officewright, at any company | Not needed |
| You are an individual using it for non-commercial purposes, a non-profit or an educational institution | Not needed |
| Your organization, together with its affiliates, had less than USD 1,000,000 in revenue in its last fiscal year, and uses Officewright inside its own product or internally (invoices, reports, contracts, statements) | Not needed; the product's documentation, about page or third-party notices must state that it uses Officewright |
| Your organization, together with its affiliates, had USD 1,000,000 or more in revenue in its last fiscal year and uses Officewright in production | Needed |
| You offer Officewright or its functionality to others as a library, SDK, API, command-line tool or hosted service for documents, paid or free, at any company size | Needed |

The table summarizes the license; where they differ, [LICENSE](LICENSE) applies.

Each version of Officewright becomes available under the Apache License 2.0 four years
after its first public release. From then on, that version needs no commercial license.

## Buying a license

Commercial licenses are not on sale yet. They will be sold through the project's
website before the first stable release.
